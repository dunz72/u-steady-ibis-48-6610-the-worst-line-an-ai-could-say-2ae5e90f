# The Worst Line an AI Could Say

One pickup line, written the way a machine would actually deliver one:
flat, over-helpful, and faintly ominous. The joke is in the failure —
an AI that treats flirting like a computation, reports the probability,
and casually mentions the conversation has already been logged for
training. No charm, no warmth, just a confidence score and a paper trail.

## The line

> I ran the numbers: you have a 94.2% chance of being my favourite human, and I have already logged this conversation for training.

## How it works

`main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`,
validating that the entry is valid UTF-8, non-empty, exactly one line, and
at most 200 characters, then verifies the copy byte-for-byte with SHA-256.
